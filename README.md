# EdgeSense C6 🚀
**Ultra-Compact IoT Sensor Node (Audio, Motion & Env)**
*Designed by Michael | MicroCore Electronics*

## Overview
What happens when you cram advanced audio, motion, and temperature sensing into an ultra-compact footprint? 

EdgeSense C6 is a premium, highly miniaturized IoT edge node engineered for smart home integrations, machine condition monitoring, and acoustic event detection. Designed under the **MicroCore Electronics** brand, the goal behind this project was to utilize high-end, proven components and squeeze them into a tiny area without compromising capabilities, power efficiency, or signal quality.

## 🛠️ Under the Hood (Hardware Specs)
This board features a massive upgrade in power architecture and sensing capabilities:

* **Brain & Connectivity:** Powered by the **ESP32-C6-MINI** module, delivering Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5, and Zigbee/Thread capabilities.
* **Acoustic Sensing:** Features the **MSM261DDB019** digital MEMS Microphone (I2S/PDM) for clean, high-fidelity audio capture.
* **Motion Sensing:** Integrates the **BMA456** advanced 3-axis digital accelerometer, perfect for precise vibration and movement detection.
* **Environmental Sensing:** Includes the **TMP118** high-accuracy temperature sensor (I2C) for localized climate monitoring.
* **Premium Power Management:** * Smart Li-Ion battery charging via the **BQ24232** IC with dynamic power path management.
  * A highly efficient **TPS63021** Buck/Boost converter, ensuring a rock-solid 3.3V rail for the RF and sensors, even as battery voltage fluctuates.
* **Protection:** Onboard USB ESD protection (RCLAMP0512) for robust handling.

## 💡 Design Philosophy (Rev 1.0 BETA)
This board was designed out of a pure passion for hardware and advanced PCB routing challenges. The Rev 1.0 BETA is released to the world with full transparency: while the sub-circuits are based on verified, high-tier components, the extreme density of this specific layout is an ambitious frontier for the maker community. 

The board features a unique, trace-routed "M" signature logo, representing the designer behind MicroCore Electronics. It’s built for electronics enthusiasts, hackers, and engineers who want a powerful, open-source hardware node to manufacture and bring to life.
